About our business

A local mill with a growing brand vision.

M/S BALARAM MURI MILL, led by proprietor Jayanti Saha, is based in Gazipur, Sahapara, Birbhum, West Bengal. BALARAM GOLD is the proposed consumer-facing brand for the mill's regular and premium muri.
